## Delta

**UI/UX depth:** touch — the shell's sidebar, top bar and content paint across every built-in style and preset

- **UI-1** — The default `page` tone paints the sidebar and the bar with the page colour in both modes (bare Flat, bare Glass, Studio Green).
- **UI-2** — Braun Panel's dark bar takes the same fill as its rail.
- **UI-3** — A veiled bar tints from the rail's fill (the flat fill under the flat shell), so the brand row and the bar meet without a tone step (Dusk Haze, Milk Glass, Aurora Night).
- **UI-4** — Under a full-width bar the rail's brand row is the rail's top row, with no bar-high band or rule under the bar (bare Tactile).
- **UI-5** — Pastel Soft and Paper Ink chrome sits a tone off the content in both modes.
- **UI-6** — Card Stock dark lays a card-coloured content sheet on a page-coloured ground.
- **UI-7** — In the island and floating shells the brand row's rule lines up with the bar's bottom edge (Soft Glass).
- **UI-8** — Table footers and the data table's sticky header keep the card surface under a toned bar.
- **UX-1** — Under the full-width bar the collapse toggle in the rail still collapses and expands it by keyboard.
- **UI-9** — At phone width the inset shell's content sheet keeps the same gutter on both sides.
- **UI-10** — In dark, the pill selection's neutral tint clears the rail on the darkest page rail (bare Flat) as well as on Quiet.
- **OP-1** — Phone width (375x812) keeps the bar-over-content layout with no horizontal overflow, light and dark.
