## Delta

**UI/UX depth:** touch — `SplitPanelLayout`'s resize handle takes `ResizableHandle withHandle`'s bar and a 12px grab strip, plus two pointer fixes on the same handle

- **UI-1** — At rest, a 4×28px rounded bar in `--foreground` at 15% opacity is centred on the panel/content boundary in all five shell styles (classic, flat, inset, island, floating). The handle takes no width and paints no wash; in classic the panel's own chrome edge stays under the bar.
- **UI-2** — Hover, keyboard focus and drag grow the bar to 56px in `--primary-fill`; the handle and its strip stay unpainted, and outside forced colours the focused handle draws no outline or ring (the bar is the indicator).
- **UI-3** — Forced colours: the bar is CanvasText at rest and Highlight on keyboard focus and drag; focus keeps the kit's 2px Highlight outline from `theme.css`.
- **UI-4** — Reduced motion: the bar changes size and colour without a transition.
- **UX-1** — The grab strip is 12px: from 2px inside the panel to 10px into the content, mirrored under `dir="rtl"`. A panel scroll bar against the edge keeps taking presses 3px and more from the edge.
- **UX-2** — Under the floating shell style the handle takes a press and drags (in 7.1.0 the content plane covered it).
- **UX-3** — A drag, by mouse or touch, moves the panel's edge by the pointer's travel from the press: no jump on the first move in any style (in 7.1.0 the panel jumped on the first move).
- **UX-4** — Arrow keys, Home and End still resize; below 768px the handle is hidden.
- **OP-1** — On `/inbox` at both viewports and themes: no axe wcag2a/wcag2aa violations, no console errors, no horizontal overflow.
