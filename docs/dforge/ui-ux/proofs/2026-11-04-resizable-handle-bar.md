## Delta

**UI/UX depth:** touch — `ResizableHandle` takes darkcloud's files-page handle: a 12px grab strip, and under `withHandle` a rounded bar that grows to the accent

- **UI-1** — `withHandle` matches darkcloud's files-page handle: at rest a 4×28px rounded bar (28×4px in a vertical group) in `--foreground` at 15% opacity, centred on a divider that draws no line outside forced colours, with no grip icon.
- **UI-2** — Hover, keyboard focus and dragging grow the bar to 56px and paint it `--primary-fill` while the divider stays transparent; the grab strip shows no wash, and outside forced colours the focus outline is replaced by the bar.
- **UI-3** — A plain handle (no `withHandle`) stays a 1px divider: hover and drag paint it `--primary-fill`, keyboard focus shows the 2px focus outline.
- **UX-1** — The grab strip is 12px wide and centred on the divider: a press 5px to either side of the line starts a drag, in both orientations, including over a positioned element inside the later panel.
- **UX-2** — Arrow keys still move the divider by the step; a disabled handle shows the not-allowed cursor, dims, and its bar does not grow on hover.
- **UI-4** — Forced colours: the divider draws a CanvasText line (Highlight while dragged) even under `withHandle`, because `theme.css` restates every structural mark in forced colours and a faint bar alone would leave no boundary; the bar is CanvasText at rest and Highlight while focused or dragged, and focus keeps the kit's 2px Highlight outline that `theme.css` gives every focused control in forced colours. Under `dir="rtl"` the bar stays centred on the divider.
- **UI-5** — Reduced motion: the bar changes size without a visible transition.
- **UI-7** — Between card panes with a 12px gap, as on darkcloud's files page, the kit handle shows the same marks as darkcloud: no line, a faint bar at rest, a 56px accent bar on hover.
- **UI-6** — Worst case: in a horizontal group 40px tall, the bar stays inside the group at rest and when grown.
- **OP-1** — Proven at 375x812 and 1280x800, light and dark, with no accessibility violations, no console errors and no horizontal overflow on the fixture and the Resizable showcase page.

## Not proven here

- Overlap with a classic scrollbar: headless Chromium draws overlay scrollbars (`scrollbarWidth` 0 in the `scroll` case), so the 6px the strip covers of a left panel's classic scrollbar could not be shown. That overlap is the accepted cost of the 12px strip.
